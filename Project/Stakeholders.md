**Exercise 4: Identify Project Stakeholders**

**Identifying all major stakeholders associated with your software project.**

|**Stakeholder**|**Role**|**Interest/Expectations**|**Influence on the System**|**How the System Affects the Stakeholder**|
|-|-|-|-|-|
|League administrators|Overseeing all league operations, game rule enforcing and scheduling.|A centralized dashboard that tracks team standings accurately, reduces manual paperwork and prevents scheduling conflicts, all in a bug-free environment.|**HIGH:** They decide the core requirements for the back end admin portal and fixture generation rules.|The system significantly reduces their administrative workload, removes human error in scheduling and is a primary point of reference for all league data.|
|Coaches/Team Owners|Representatives responsible for registering individual teams and managing their rosters.|Easy team registration, immediate access to match schedules and straightforward roster updates like setting lineups.|**MEDIUM:** They specify the requirements for team management and UI components.|Removes the need to submit physical registration forms, provides real-time updates on match logistics|
|Players|Participants competing in the community sports league.|Easy account creation, ability to view their match times and tracking personal/team performance on leaderboards.|**LOW**: Since they are the primary end users, their usage of the system will decide its operational success.|Gives direct, real-time access to match timings, standings, schedules without constantly relying on the coach for updates.|
|Viewers/Fans|People following the league's progress and supporting teams.|Quick and easy access to upcoming fixtures, live scores and leaderboards|**LOW:** They only interact with the read only views and information.|Enhances their viewing experience by providing a centralized digital source for all league information.|
|Development Team|Software Engineers coding, testing and documenting the SpotFix platform.|Successfully delivering project requirements within the timeline and building a robust, working prototype.|**HIGH:** They have complete control over software architecture, database design, tech stack and feature implementation.|Requires a good amount of time investment and technical application. The system's success directly depends on them.|
