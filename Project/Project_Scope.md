Exercise 2: Define the Project Scope
Define the initial scope of your software project.
Your project scope should include:
===================================================================================================================================
1.	Project Objective
Provide a clear statement describing the main purpose of the project and the problem it aims to address.
The objective of this project is to design and develop SpotFix,  a centralized, platform for managing and catering to community sports leagues. The system addresses the scheduling errors, inefficient team/player registrations, and delays in updating match fixtures and standings when done manually. A platform to automate and organize such things is what we are aiming to create.  
===================================================================================================================================
2.	Target Users
Identify the main users or beneficiaries of the proposed system.
1.	League administrators who will be in charge of game rules, management, scheduling, registration etc. 
2.	Team owners/ coaches who will be registering their own teams.
3.	The viewers/general users who come to view match schedules, match results and team rankings
===================================================================================================================================
3.	In-Scope Features
List the major features and functionalities that will be included in the project.
1.	Account creation and designation of account types (Different account types give different controls/features).
2.	Team management of starting line ups, substitutes and adding/removal of players.
3.	Registration and updating of individual accounts as a player. 
4.	Automated Match schedule generations
5.	Score and leaderboard tracking
6.	In-app and other methods (via SMS or email) of notifications 
===================================================================================================================================
4.	Out-of-Scope Features
Identify features or functionalities that will not be included in the current version of the project.
1.	Sale of Team Merchandise, meet and greet event organizations.
2.	Supporting multiple languages
3.	Player Equipment Registration
===================================================================================================================================
5.	Major Deliverables
Identify the main expected deliverables of the project, such as software components, documentation, prototypes, or other project artifacts.
1.	Final web application/final product.
2.	User Manual/ Instructions.
3.	Test results, progress updates and reports.
