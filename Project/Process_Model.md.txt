The Incremental Model

Justification:

Successive increments add new features

After the main registration is successfully implemented, the complex real time match scheduling can be developed as add-ons

As early stages of the software are rolled out, we can get early customer feedback making it easier to understand what the users want 

Major Drawbacks:

it would require major upfront detailing, we would have to be very forward thinking with the structure of the database such that future data of the scheduling do not make us rebuild the early foundation of the project 

Integration of increments may increase costs and time of development, after each increment we would need to be very rigorous with the integration testing and have the foresight of future increments when we make the previous increments
